Key Career Highlights

About Lewis Hamilton

Born on January 7, 1985, in Stevenage, England, Lewis Hamilton began his motorsport journey at a young age. His innate talent quickly propelled him through the ranks, leading to his highly anticipated Formula 1 debut in 2007.

Hamilton's career is marked by incredible determination, skill, and a commitment to pushing boundaries, both on and off the track. He continues to be a formidable competitor and a powerful voice for social change.
